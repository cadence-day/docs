# Notes

Notes let you add a few words to an hour: what happened, how it felt, what you want to remember.

## Writing a note

Every note belongs to an hour. You can write one:

- **In Log, swipe up** on the wheel of hours. A **Note** sheet opens for the hour in the center.
- **In Log, tap +** in the toolbar.
- **In the Calendar's Day scale, swipe left** on an hour.
- **In an hour's details**, tap **Write a note**.

<!-- screenshot: NN-note-sheet -->

A note can be up to 3,000 characters.

## Reading your notes

Notes appear in the details of their hour: tap the hour in the Calendar's Day scale. There is no separate list of all notes yet, and notes can't be searched yet.

## Are notes private?

Yes. **Note text is encrypted on your device** before it is saved, so only your devices can read it. The time a note belongs to is not encrypted. See [Encryption & Security](/docs/faq/encryption).
