# Logging Your Time

Cadence works in hours. For each hour of your day, you pick the activity that best describes it. Over time, those colored hours become a picture of how you actually live.

## The Log tab

**Log** is where you record your day.

![Log: the wheel of hours with the activity list below, asking "What did you do at 3?"](/img/app/log-select-hour.webp)

- At the top is the date. Tap it to pick another day (up to today).
- In the middle is a wheel of the day's hours.
- Below it, Cadence asks **"What did you do at 14:00?"** and lists your activities.

To log an hour:

1. Scroll the wheel so the hour you want is in the center.
2. Tap an activity in the list.

That's it. Cadence moves on to the next hour. When every hour of the day is filled, you'll see **Finish my day** and your current streak, and you can share an image of your day.

You can only log hours that have already happened. Cadence doesn't plan ahead.

## Activities

Activities are the categories you log your hours into, such as Work, Sleep, Exercise or Friends. You pick your first ones during onboarding, and you can change them any time.

Go to **Settings → Tracking → Activities**:

![Settings, Activities: the list of active activities with drag handles](/img/app/settings-activities.webp)

- **Add** an activity: give it a name, a **Color** and an **Icon**.
- **Edit** one: tap it to see its hours and weekly pattern, and change its name, color or icon.
- **Reorder** them: drag to reorder. The order is the order of the list in Log.
- **Disable Activity**: hides it from logging but keeps its history. Disabled activities are listed under **Disabled Activities**; tap one to reactivate it.
- **Delete Activity**: if it has logged hours, Cadence asks which activity to move them to. Deleted activities can be restored from **Deleted Activities**.

You can also reach the editor from the activity list in Log.

Activity names are encrypted on your device. See [Encryption & Security](/docs/faq/encryption).

## Details of an hour

Tap an hour in the Calendar's Day scale to see everything about it:

- the activity;
- **Trackers**: entries for that hour, such as mood (swipe a row to delete it);
- your **notes**, with **Write a note** to add one;
- **Health** readings (heart rate, steps) and a **map** of where you were, if you turned on those [extensions](/docs/features/extensions).
