# Calendar

The Calendar tab shows everything you've logged, from a whole year down to a single day. Its label in the tab bar follows the scale you're looking at: **Year**, **Week** or **Day**.

<!-- screenshot: NN-calendar-year -->

## Three scales: Year, Week, Day

- **Year**: a compact list of weeks. Each day is a thin row of 24 hours, colored by the activity you logged in each hour. It's the quickest way to see your rhythm over months.
- **Week**: the same weeks, taller, with week headings and weekday labels. Here you can open the **Modules** drawer (**Show modules**) to see your daily modules, such as mood or steps, next to each day.
- **Day**: one day as a list of hours, with a strip of the week's dates on top.

### Switching scales

- **Pinch**: spread two fingers to zoom in (Year → Week → Day) and pinch to zoom out. The view snaps to the nearest scale when you let go.
- **Menu**: use the menu in the top-right corner and pick **Year**, **Week** or **Day**.
- **Tap a day**: choose **View** to open it in Day, or **Edit** to change it.

## Moving through time

- Scroll up to go further back. Your history goes back as far as you've logged.
- In Day, drag from the left or right edge of the screen to go to the previous or next day.
- Tap **Today** to jump back to the present.

**Today is always the newest day.** Cadence records what happened, not what's planned, so there are no future days to scroll to and no planning ahead.

## In the Day scale

- **Tap an hour** to open its details: the activity, tracker entries, notes, and, if you turned them on, Health readings and a small map.
- **Swipe left on an hour** to write a note for it.

See [Logging Your Time](/docs/features/activity-tracking) and [Notes](/docs/features/note-taking).

## Is there a Month view?

No. The Calendar has Year, Week and Day. To look at a month of numbers, use the **Analytics** tab and pick **Month** as the period. See [Analytics](/docs/features/analytics).

## Where does the app open?

**Settings → General → Open on** decides whether Cadence opens on the Calendar or on **Log**.
