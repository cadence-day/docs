# Timeline & Calendar

Master Cadence's visual timeline interface and calendar system for intuitive time tracking.

## The Timeline

The Timeline is the heart of Cadence—a visual, interactive interface for logging and reviewing how you spend your time.

### Visual Design

**24-Hour Grid System:**
- Each day divided into 48 time slots (30 minutes each)
- Color-coded activity blocks for instant recognition
- Vertical scrolling through hours of the day
- Horizontal swiping between days

**Activity Blocks:**
- Colored rectangles representing logged activities
- Visual continuity for extended activity periods
- Quick-tap to view or edit
- Add notes directly to any time slot

### Two Timeline Modes

Cadence offers two distinct timeline visualization modes:

#### Circular Timeline

**Visual Metaphor**: A clock-inspired circular view of your day

**Features:**
- 24-hour clock face layout
- Activities arranged radially around the circle
- Visual representation of your daily rhythm
- Intuitive "time of day" understanding
- Rotating view follows current time

**Best for:**
- Understanding daily patterns at a glance
- Seeing how activities flow throughout the day
- Visual learners who think in "clock time"
- Quick daily overview

#### Linear Timeline

**Visual Metaphor**: A traditional vertical timeline

**Features:**
- Top-to-bottom chronological layout
- Scrollable vertical list of time blocks
- Precise time slot visibility
- Detailed activity information
- Easy batch editing

**Best for:**
- Detailed logging and editing
- Reviewing specific time periods
- Users preferring traditional list views
- Granular time management

**Switch between modes:** Toggle in settings or swipe gestures to find your preferred view.

**Within a Day:**
- **Scroll vertically**: Navigate through hours

**Calendar Picker:**
- Visual calendar with completion gauges on each day

## The Calendar

The Calendar system provides date navigation and visual insights into your tracking consistency.

### Calendar Dialog

**Features:**
- **Monthly view**: Full month calendar layout
- **Completion gauges**: Circular progress border on each day
- **Gauge visualization**: 0-100% based on timeslices logged
- **Theme integration**: Colors match your app theme
- **Quick navigation**: Swipe between months

**Understanding Gauges:**
- Each day can have up to 48 timeslices (30 min each)
- Gauge shows percentage tracked
- **Example**: 24 timeslices = 50% completion
- Visual feedback on tracking consistency

**Interactive Features:**
- **Tap to select**: Choose a date to view its timeline
- **Double-tap to reset**: Recenter gauge at midnight (top)
- **Swipe months**: Navigate forward/backward
- **Disabled future dates**: Grayed out and non-interactive

## Time Slot System

### Understanding Time Slots

**Granularity:**
- Each slot represents 30 minutes
- 48 slots per day (24 hours)
- Slots span from midnight to midnight
- Can log multiple activities per slot (overlapping features coming)

**Slot States:**
- **Empty**: Untracked time (no activity)
- **Filled**: Activity logged for that slot
- **With note**: Activity plus contextual note
- **Current**: The current 30-minute period (today only)

### Best Practices

**When to Log:**

**Real-time logging:**
- Most accurate but requires discipline
- Log as you transition between activities
- Use notifications as reminders

**Batch logging:**
- Evening review of the day
- Use calendar/memory as reference
- Faster but may miss details

**Hybrid approach (recommended):**
- Log major activities in real-time
- Fill gaps during evening review
- Best balance of accuracy and convenience

**How Detailed:**

**Sufficient detail:**
- 1-2 hour blocks of focused work
- 30-minute blocks for varied activities
- Capture actual activity changes

**Too detailed:**
- Switching activities every 15 minutes
- Logging bathroom breaks
- Excessive granularity (exhausting to maintain)

**Too broad:**
- Entire day as "work"
- Missing important transitions
- Insufficient data for insights

## Troubleshooting

### Timeline not loading

**Check connection:**
- Ensure internet connectivity
- Refresh with pull-down gesture
- Restart the app

**Clear cache:**
- Sign out and back in
- Reinstall if persistent

### Activities not saving

**Verify account:**
- Ensure you're signed in
- Check internet connection
- Look for error messages

**Check permissions:**
- Storage permissions enabled
- Background refresh allowed

### Calendar gauges incorrect

**Data sync:**
- Pull down to refresh calendar
- Check if all timeslices synced
- Review the specific day's timeline

**Calculation:**
- Gauge = (logged timeslices / 48) × 100%
- Verify expected completion percentage

### Can't navigate to certain dates

**Future dates:**
- Future dates intentionally disabled
- Can't log what hasn't happened

**Past dates:**
- Should all be accessible
- Report bug if limited access
