# Sage AI Chat

Meet Sage, your intelligent companion for understanding your time and discovering patterns in how you live.

## What is Sage?

Sage is an AI-powered chat assistant built into Cadence that transforms your time tracking data into conversational insights. Instead of manually analyzing your activities, simply ask Sage questions in natural language and get intelligent answers based on your real data.

Think of Sage as your personal data analyst who:

- **Knows your history**: Has access to all your tracked activities and notes
- **Understands patterns**: Can identify trends you might miss
- **Speaks naturally**: No complicated queries or technical knowledge needed
- **Protects your privacy**: Processes data securely with end-to-end encryption

## Core Capabilities

### Natural Language Queries

Ask questions the way you naturally think:

- "What did I do yesterday afternoon?"
- "How much time did I spend working last week?"
- "When do I usually exercise?"
- "What patterns do you see in my daily routine?"

No need to learn query syntax or navigate complex menus.

### Data Access & Analysis

Sage has access to powerful tools that retrieve and analyze:

#### Activity Data
- All your tracked activities with names, categories, and colors
- Specific activities by ID or name
- Activity frequency and duration patterns

#### Time Slices
- Detailed timeslice records for any date or date range
- Time spent on each activity
- Gaps in your tracking

#### Notes & Context
- All your notes attached to activities
- Specific notes by ID or content
- Contextual information about your days

#### States & Metadata
- Activity states and status
- Custom metadata you've added
- Historical changes and trends

### Intelligent Insights

Sage doesn't just retrieve data—it analyzes it:

- **Pattern Recognition**: Identifies recurring patterns in your schedule
- **Time Analysis**: Calculates totals, averages, and distributions
- **Comparisons**: Compares time periods (this week vs. last week)
- **Contextual Understanding**: Combines activities and notes for richer insights

## How to Use Sage

### Getting Started

1. **Open the Chat tab**: Find Sage in the bottom navigation between Reflection and Profile
2. **Say hello**: Start with a simple question like "What activities did I log today?"
3. **Have a conversation**: Ask follow-up questions—Sage remembers the context
4. **Get insights**: Learn about your patterns and habits through natural dialogue

### Example Conversations

**Understanding Your Day:**

> **You**: "What did I do today?"
>
> **Sage**: "Today you logged 8 hours of work, 1 hour of exercise, 30 minutes of cooking, and 2 hours of family time. You also added notes during your morning meeting mentioning the Q1 planning discussion."
>
> **You**: "How does that compare to yesterday?"
>
> **Sage**: "You worked 2 hours more today than yesterday, but spent the same amount of time on exercise. Yesterday you had more personal time in the evening."

**Discovering Patterns:**

> **You**: "When do I typically exercise?"
>
> **Sage**: "Based on the last 30 days, you most often exercise in the morning between 7-9am (60% of the time), with occasional afternoon sessions around 5pm (30%). Your most consistent days are Monday, Wednesday, and Friday."

**Work-Life Analysis:**

> **You**: "How much have I worked this week?"
>
> **Sage**: "This week you've logged 42 hours of work across Monday through Friday. Your longest day was Wednesday with 9.5 hours, and you took a proper lunch break every day except Tuesday."

## Advanced Features

### Contextual Follow-ups

Sage maintains conversation context, allowing natural follow-up questions:

```
You: "What activities did I log last week?"
Sage: [Lists activities with hours]
You: "Which day had the most work time?"
Sage: "Wednesday had the most work time with 9 hours..."
You: "What notes did I add that day?"
Sage: "On Wednesday you added notes about..."
```

### Multi-criteria Analysis

Ask complex questions combining multiple dimensions:

- "Show me all days last month where I exercised AND worked less than 6 hours"
- "What activities do I do most often on weekends vs. weekdays?"
- "When I add notes about feeling energized, what activities was I doing?"

### Time-based Queries

Sage understands flexible time expressions:

- "today", "yesterday", "last week", "this month"
- "Monday", "last Tuesday", "three days ago"
- Specific dates: "October 15th", "the first week of November"
- Date ranges: "from October 1 to October 7"

**Apple Intelligence (iOS 18+)**

- Lower latency for instant insights

**Anthropic Claude 3.5 Sonnet**
- State-of-the-art language model

- Streaming responses for better UX

## Best Practices

### Ask Specific Questions

**Less effective**: "How was last week?"
**More effective**: "How many hours did I work last week compared to my average?"

**Less effective**: "Tell me about exercise"
**More effective**: "How often did I exercise in the last 30 days and what time of day?"

### Use Date Ranges

Being specific helps Sage provide better answers:

- "Show me my work hours for the first two weeks of November"
- "Compare my exercise frequency in October vs. September"

### Combine Data Sources

Get richer insights by asking about activities AND notes:

- "On days I noted feeling productive, what was my schedule like?"
- "What activities preceded notes about feeling tired?"

### Iterate and Refine

Don't hesitate to ask follow-up questions:

1. Start broad: "What did I do last week?"
2. Narrow down: "Tell me more about my work activities"
3. Get specific: "What meetings did I have on Tuesday?"

## Troubleshooting

### Sage isn't showing up

**Check feature availability:**
- The Chat feature may be controlled by a feature flag
- Ensure you have the latest app version
- Check if your subscription plan includes Sage

**Restart the app:**
- Close completely and reopen
- Check the bottom navigation for the Chat tab

### Responses are inaccurate

**Improve data quality:**
- Ensure activities are tracked consistently
- Use clear, consistent activity names
- Add relevant notes for context

**Rephrase questions:**
- Be more specific with dates and time ranges
- Use exact activity names from your list
- Break complex questions into simpler parts

### Slow or no responses

**Check internet connection:**
- Sage requires internet (except Apple Intelligence for some queries)
- Try again with better connectivity

**Simplify query:**
- Ask about shorter time ranges
- Focus on specific activities
- Reduce complexity

### Privacy concerns

**Review what Sage accesses:**
- Only Cadence data (activities, notes, timeslices)
- No access to other apps or device data
- No permanent storage of conversations
- End-to-end encrypted transmission
