# Activities

## What are activities?

Activities are what you log your hours into: Work, Sleep, Exercise, Friends, and so on. Each one has a name, a color and an icon. You choose your first ones during onboarding.

## How do I log an activity?

Open **Log**, scroll the wheel so the hour is in the center, and tap an activity under **"What did you do at …?"**. See [Logging Your Time](/docs/features/activity-tracking).

## Can I log hours in the past?

Yes. In **Log**, tap the date at the top and pick an earlier day. You can't log hours that haven't happened yet.

## How do I create, edit or reorder activities?

Go to **Settings → Tracking → Activities**. Add a new one, tap one to edit its name, **Color** or **Icon**, or drag to reorder.

## Can I temporarily disable an activity?

Yes. Open the activity and tap **Disable Activity**. It disappears from the list in Log but keeps its history. Tap it under **Disabled Activities** to reactivate it.

## What happens when I delete an activity?

If it has logged hours, Cadence asks which activity to move them to. Deleted activities are listed under **Deleted Activities**, where you can restore them.

## Can I see how much time I spend on one activity?

Yes. Tap the activity in **Settings → Tracking → Activities** to see its hours and weekly pattern, or use the [Analytics](/docs/features/analytics) tab.

## Are activity names private?

Yes. Activity names are encrypted on your device. Colors and icons are not. See [Encryption & Security](/docs/faq/encryption).

## Can I export my activity data?

**Settings → General → Export all data** downloads the data in your Cadence account as a JSON file. With Cloud sync off, that's only what was synced before. See [Cloud Sync & Devices](/docs/faq/sync-and-devices).
