# Insights

Learn about Cadence's insights and analytics features.

## What are Insights?

Insights in Cadence help you understand patterns in how you spend your time. They transform raw activity data into meaningful visualizations and metrics that reveal trends, habits, and opportunities for improvement.

## What insights are available?

### Activity Distribution

See how you spend your time across different activities:

- **Pie charts**: Visual breakdown of time allocation
- **Percentages**: Exact time distribution
- **Time totals**: Hours and minutes per activity
- **Category summaries**: Time grouped by activity categories

### Time Analysis

Understand your temporal patterns:

- **Daily patterns**: When you do specific activities
- **Weekly trends**: Activity frequency by day of week
- **Monthly overview**: Long-term habit tracking
- **Streak tracking**: Consistency in daily logging

### Calendar Visualization

Visual representations of your data:

- **Monthly calendar**: See completion gauges for each day
- **Streak indicators**: Track consecutive days of logging
- **Heat maps**: Identify busy vs. quiet periods
- **Gap detection**: Spot missing data or untracked time

## How do I access Insights?

Insights are integrated throughout the app:

1. **Reflection Screen**: View weekly patterns and activity summaries
2. **Calendar Dialog**: See daily completion gauges on each date
3. **Profile**: Access streak information and statistics
4. **Timeline**: Visual indicators of daily progress

## What is the daily completion gauge?

The daily completion gauge shows how much of your day you've tracked:

- **Calculation**: Based on 48 possible 30-minute timeslices per day
- **Visual indicator**: Circular progress ring around calendar days
- **Percentage**: 0-100% completion based on logged activities
- **Color-coded**: Uses your theme colors for consistency

For example:
- 24 timeslices logged = 50% completion
- 48 timeslices logged = 100% completion

## What is the streak calendar?

The streak calendar tracks your consistency:

- **Daily tracking**: Shows which days you've logged activities
- **Consecutive days**: Highlights continuous logging periods
- **Visual motivation**: See your progress at a glance
- **Historical view**: Review past months of tracking

Data export functionality is coming soon! Future versions will allow you to:

## How far back can I view insights?

You can view insights for any time period where you have tracked activities:

- **No time limit**: Access all historical data
- **Flexible ranges**: Day, week, month, custom periods
- **Year-over-year**: Compare across different time periods

## Are insights available offline?

Basic insights based on locally cached data are available offline. Cloud-synced insights and advanced analytics require an internet connection.

## How accurate are the insights?

Insight accuracy depends on your tracking consistency:

**Highly accurate when:**
- ✅ You log activities daily
- ✅ You track most of your waking hours
- ✅ You use consistent activity names
- ✅ You categorize activities correctly

**Less reliable when:**
- ❌ Many days have missing data
- ❌ Only tracking partial days
- ❌ Inconsistent activity naming
- ❌ Irregular logging patterns

**Pro tip**: Aim to log at least 75% of your waking hours for meaningful insights.

## What's the difference between Insights and Reflections?

**Insights** are data-driven:
- Automatic calculations and visualizations
- Objective metrics and statistics
- Pattern detection through analysis
- Quantitative understanding

**Reflections** are personal:
- Your thoughts and observations
- Subjective experiences and feelings
- Written synthesis of your week
- Qualitative understanding

Both complement each other for a complete picture!

## Troubleshooting

### No insights showing

**Check your data:**
- Ensure you've logged activities
- Verify data for the selected time period
- Confirm activities are properly categorized

**Refresh the view:**
- Pull down to refresh
- Switch to a different time period and back
- Restart the app

### Insights seem wrong

**Review your data:**
- Check for duplicate activities
- Verify correct activity times
- Ensure proper categorization

**Report issues:**
- Profile → Customer Support → Bug Report
- Include screenshots of unexpected insights
- Describe what you expected vs. what you saw

### Slow loading insights

**For large datasets:**
- Insights may take a few seconds to calculate
- Try shorter time ranges
- Ensure good internet connection for cloud features
