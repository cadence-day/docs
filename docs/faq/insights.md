# Analytics

## Where do I find my stats?

In the **Analytics** tab. Pick a period (**Week**, **Month**, **Quarter**, **Year** or **Custom**) and swipe or use the arrows to move between periods. See [Analytics](/docs/features/analytics).

## What does the heatmap show?

**Days with Activity** shows the past 12 weeks. Darker cells mean more hours logged that day. It always covers the last 12 weeks, whatever period you pick.

## Can I choose which charts I see?

Yes. Tap **Customize widgets** to add, remove and reorder them.

## Does Analytics work offline or with Cloud sync off?

Most widgets are calculated on your iPhone and work in local mode. Some comparisons are computed on Cadence servers and need Cloud sync on to include everything you logged.

## Why do my numbers look low?

Analytics counts the hours you logged. Empty hours aren't counted. Fill in past days in **Log** to complete the picture.
