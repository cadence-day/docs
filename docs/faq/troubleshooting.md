# Troubleshooting

## My data isn't on my other device

Check three things:

1. **Cloud sync** is on, on both devices (Settings → General). With it off, data stays on the iPhone it was recorded on.
2. Both devices are signed in to the **same account**.
3. The new device is **linked**, so it can read activity names and notes (Settings → Encryption → Link new device).

Multi-device sync is still being finished, so a change made on two devices at once may not show up on both. See [Cloud Sync & Devices](/docs/faq/sync-and-devices).

## Sync could not finish

Settings → General shows how many items are still waiting. Check your connection and tap **Sync again**. Your data is safe on your iPhone in the meantime.

## I see "Unable to decrypt"

This device doesn't have the key the text was encrypted with. Link it to a device that has your data. See [Encryption & Security](/docs/faq/encryption#why-do-i-see-unable-to-decrypt).

## I can't sign in

- Use the same method you signed up with (Apple, Google, or email and password).
- For email, use **Forgot password** to reset it.
- Check your connection: signing in always needs one.

## The map or Health readings are empty

- Check that **Location**, **Heart rate** or **Steps** are on in Settings → Extensions.
- For location gaps, allow **Always** access and avoid force-quitting Cadence.
- For Health, check **Health → Sharing → Apps → Cadence**.

See [Location & Health](/docs/faq/location-health).

## Times look wrong

Check **Settings → General → Time style** and **Start week on**.

## I can't delete my account

Delete it in **Settings → Account → Delete account**, with a connection. If it fails, email [admin@cadence.day](mailto:admin@cadence.day).

## Export doesn't contain everything

**Export all data** (Settings → General) exports your Cadence account. If Cloud sync is off, data that is only on your iPhone isn't in it. Location and Health history are exported separately from **Settings → Extensions → Export data**.

## How do I report a bug?

Email [admin@cadence.day](mailto:admin@cadence.day) with what happened, what you expected, your iPhone model and the app version (Settings → About & Legal), or post in the [Cadence Discord](https://discord.gg/cW9gCRA6Kn).
