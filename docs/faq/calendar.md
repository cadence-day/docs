# Calendar & Timeline

Learn how to navigate your activities across time in Cadence.

## How does the timeline view work?

The timeline is your main interface for tracking activities:

- **Time slots**: Each slot represents a 30-minute period (48 per day)
- **Color-coded blocks**: Activities shown as colored rectangles
- **Multiple views**: Choose between Circular or Linear timeline
- **Scrollable**: Scroll vertically through hours, horizontally between days
- **Tap to log**: Tap any time slot to log an activity
- **Auto-save**: All changes save automatically

The timeline gives you a visual, intuitive representation of how you spend your time.

## What are the different timeline views?

Cadence offers two timeline visualization modes:

### Circular Timeline

**Clock-inspired radial view:**

- 24-hour clock face layout
- Activities arranged around the circle
- Intuitive time-of-day understanding
- Rotating view follows current time
- Perfect for visual overview

### Linear Timeline

**Traditional vertical timeline:**

- Top-to-bottom chronological layout
- Scrollable list of time blocks
- Detailed activity information
- Easy editing and note-taking
- Precise time slot visibility

**Switch between views** in Settings or with swipe gestures.

## What is the calendar with completion gauges?

The calendar dialog provides visual feedback on your tracking consistency:

**Features:**

- **Monthly view**: Full month calendar layout
- **Completion gauges**: Circular progress ring on each day
- **Percentage tracking**: Shows 0-100% based on logged timeslices
- **Visual motivation**: See your tracking consistency at a glance
- **Theme integration**: Gauges use your app theme colors

**Understanding the gauge:**

- Each day has 48 possible 30-minute timeslices
- Gauge percentage = (logged timeslices / 48) × 100%
- Example: 24 logged timeslices = 50% completion
- Full circle = 100% (entire day logged)

**Interactive features:**

- Tap any date to view its timeline
- Double-tap a day to recenter gauge at midnight
- Swipe to navigate between months
- Future dates are disabled (grayed out)

## Can I change the time slot duration?

Currently, time slots are fixed at 30 minutes each, providing a good balance between:

- **Granularity**: Enough detail without being overwhelming
- **Simplicity**: Easy to track and review
- **Standard**: Aligns with common scheduling practices (half-hour increments)

**Why 30 minutes?**

- Most activities naturally fall into 30-minute increments
- Not too detailed (exhausting to maintain)
- Not too broad (loses meaningful patterns)
- 48 slots per day is easy to visualize (0-100% completion)

**Future options:**
We're exploring customizable time slots based on user feedback. Potential options may include:

- 15-minute slots for detailed tracking
- 1-hour slots for simpler tracking
- Flexible duration per activity type

## How do I navigate between days?

Cadence offers multiple navigation methods for flexibility:

**Swipe:**

- Swipe left/right on the timeline to move between days

**Calendar picker:**

1. Tap the calendar icon in the header
2. Select any date
3. The timeline updates to show that day

**Today button:**

- Tap the "Today" button to jump back to the current day

## Can I view my week at a glance?

Yes! The **Reflection** screen shows your week in a compact cadence view:

- See all 7 days side-by-side
- Quickly identify patterns and rhythms
- Tap any day to see details

## Does Cadence integrate with my phone's calendar?

Not yet! Calendar integration is a planned feature that will allow you to:

- Import events from your phone's calendar
- See meetings and appointments alongside your activities
- Automatically suggest activities based on calendar events

This feature is coming soon based on user demand.

## Can I view multiple days side-by-side?

The Reflection screen shows your weekly cadence in a grid view. We're exploring additional multi-day views for deeper pattern analysis.

## How far back can I view my data?

You can view your activity history from the day you started using Cadence with no limits! All your data is preserved and accessible.

## Can I set recurring activities?

Recurring activities aren't directly supported yet, but you can:

- Quickly log the same activity for multiple days
- Use patterns from your Reflection view to spot regular activities

Automated recurring activities are on our roadmap!

## What's the difference between the Timeline and Reflection views?

**Timeline (Home screen):**

- Detailed view of a single day
- Log and edit activities in real-time
- See minute-by-minute breakdown

**Reflection:**

- Weekly overview with pattern insights
- Analyze your rhythms and balance
- See total time spent per activity

Use Timeline for daily logging, Reflection for understanding patterns.

## Can I zoom in/out on the timeline?

The timeline is optimized for 30-minute increments. We're considering zoom features for future updates to support different levels of detail.

## How do I see what I did last week?

1. Tap the calendar icon
2. Select a date from last week
3. Or swipe right multiple times to go back day-by-day

The Reflection screen also shows your full week at once!
