# Calendar

## What does the Calendar show?

Every hour you've logged, colored by activity, at three scales: **Year**, **Week** and **Day**. The tab is labelled with the scale you're on. See [Calendar](/docs/features/timeline-calendar).

## How do I switch between Year, Week and Day?

Pinch to zoom in or out, or use the menu in the top-right corner. You can also tap a day and choose **View**.

## Is there a Month view?

No. The Calendar has Year, Week and Day. For monthly numbers, pick **Month** in the [Analytics](/docs/features/analytics) tab.

## Why can't I see next week?

Cadence records what happened, not what's planned. Today is always the newest day, so there are no future days to show or plan.

## How far back can I go?

Scroll up for older weeks. Your whole history is there.

## How do I get back to today?

Tap **Today**.

## How do I see what I did in a given hour?

In the Day scale, tap the hour. Its details show the activity, tracker entries, notes, and Health and map information if you turned them on.

## Does Cadence connect to my phone's calendar?

No.

## Can I choose where the app opens?

Yes: **Settings → General → Open on**.
