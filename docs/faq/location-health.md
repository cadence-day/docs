# Location & Health

## Why does Cadence ask for "Always" location access?

Only if you turn on **Location** in **Settings → Extensions**. Location records where each hour of your day happened, so it can show on a map. With **While Using the App**, Cadence records only while it's open. **Always** lets it keep recording in the background, so the map is complete even for hours you didn't open the app.

Saying no to **Always** is fine: recording just happens only while the app is open. You can change it any time in iOS Settings, or tap **Open Settings** in Settings → Extensions.

## Does Cadence upload my location?

No. Location history is stored **only on your iPhone** and is never sent to Cadence, even with Cloud sync on. The map itself is drawn with Apple Maps.

## How precise is it?

Coarse. Cadence doesn't use navigation-grade precision. It groups your location into places where you stayed and journeys between them.

## Why does it ask for Motion & Fitness?

Motion helps tell a journey apart from time spent in one place, so Cadence can record far less location. You can decline; journeys may then be recorded less precisely.

## What Health data do you read?

Only **heart rate** and **steps**, as hourly values, and only if you turn them on in **Settings → Extensions**. Cadence never writes anything to Apple Health.

iOS shows a "write" description for Cadence because the app holds the Health permission, but Cadence doesn't write any data to Health.

## Is my Health data uploaded or shared?

No. It's stored only on your iPhone, never uploaded to Cadence, not part of Cloud sync, never sold, and never used for ads.

## How do I delete my location or Health history?

**Settings → Extensions → Delete location history** or **Delete health history**. Turning the toggles off only stops recording; it keeps what's already stored.

## How do I move it to a new iPhone?

**Settings → Extensions → Export data** saves it to a file protected with a passphrase you choose. On the new iPhone, use **Import data**. Cadence can't recover the passphrase if you forget it.

## Why is an hour missing on the map?

Probably because background recording was paused: Cadence was force-quit, or **Always** access is off. Open Cadence to resume recording.
