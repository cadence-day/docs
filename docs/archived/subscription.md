# Subscription & Pricing

Everything you need to know about Cadence pricing and premium features.

## Is Cadence free?

Yes! Cadence offers a free plan with all core features:

**Free Explorer:**

- Track time in 30-minute intervals
- Add quick notes and mood tags
- Basic daily visualizations
- Community forum access
- Full day tracking (48 time slots)

We also offer subscription plans to support development and unlock premium features.

## What subscription plans are available?

Cadence offers several support tiers:

### Supporter - $4.99/month or $50/year

**Community & Influence:**

- Everything in Free Explorer
- Discord community access
- See features being considered
- Input & discussion on roadmap
- Early look at in-development features
- Supporter badge in community
- AI insights with Sage

### Premium Supporter - $9.99/month

**Beta Access & Prioritization:**

- Everything in Supporter
- Beta/TestFlight access
- Priority notice of updates
- Full roadmap visibility
- Feature prioritization influence
- Direct feedback channel
- Data export capabilities

### Feature Sponsor - $50 one-time

**Shape Cadence's Future:**

- Suggest one feature or integration
- Direct engagement on implementation
- Feature gets prioritized/built
- Permanent sponsor recognition
- Lifetime supporter community access
- All Premium Supporter benefits

Pricing may vary by region. Check the app for exact pricing in your currency.

## Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes! You can cancel anytime:

1. Open iOS **Settings**
2. Tap your name at the top
3. Tap **Subscriptions**
4. Find **Cadence** in the list
5. Tap **Cancel Subscription**

You'll retain Premium access until the end of your billing period.

## What happens if I cancel Premium?

If you cancel your subscription:

- **Premium features** will be disabled at the end of your billing period
- **Your data remains safe** - nothing is deleted
- **Core features** continue working (tracking, notes, basic visualizations)
- **You can resubscribe** anytime to regain premium access
- **Supporter community access** ends (except for Feature Sponsors who have lifetime access)

## Can I switch between monthly and yearly plans?

Yes! To switch between Supporter monthly and yearly plans:

1. Cancel your current subscription
2. Wait for it to expire
3. Subscribe to the new plan

The App Store doesn't allow direct plan switching, but this process is straightforward. The yearly plan offers significant savings (17% off).

**Note**: Switching between different tiers (e.g., Supporter to Premium Supporter) follows the same process.

## Do you offer student discounts?

Not currently, but we're exploring student and nonprofit pricing! Contact us at admin@cadence.day if you're interested.

## Is there a lifetime option?

Yes! The **Feature Sponsor** tier is a one-time $50 payment that gives you:

- Lifetime supporter community access
- All Premium Supporter benefits
- Opportunity to suggest and help prioritize a feature
- Permanent sponsor recognition

For recurring subscriptions, the yearly Supporter plan at $50/year offers the best value with 17% savings vs. monthly.

## Can I get a refund?

Refund requests are handled by Apple through the App Store:

1. Go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)
2. Sign in with your Apple ID
3. Find your Cadence purchase
4. Select "Request a refund"
5. Choose your reason and submit

Apple reviews refund requests on a case-by-case basis.

## What payment methods do you accept?

Cadence uses App Store (iOS) and Google Play (Android) billing, so you can use:

**iOS (App Store):**

- Credit/debit cards
- Apple Pay
- Apple ID balance
- Mobile carrier billing (if supported)
- Any payment method linked to your Apple ID

**Android (Google Play):**

- Credit/debit cards
- Google Pay
- Google Play balance
- PayPal (in some regions)
- Mobile carrier billing (if supported)
- Any payment method linked to your Google account

## Will my subscription auto-renew?

Yes, subscriptions auto-renew by default:

- **Monthly**: Renews every month
- **Yearly**: Renews every year

You can disable auto-renewal in your App Store subscription settings. You'll still have access until the end of your current billing period.

## Can I share Premium with family?

Family Sharing for Cadence subscriptions is coming soon! This will allow one subscription to cover multiple family members using Apple's Family Sharing feature.

Currently, subscriptions are linked to individual Apple IDs. Stay tuned for updates on family sharing availability!

## Is Support worth it?

That depends on your needs! Here's what each tier offers:

**Free Explorer** is perfect if you:

- Want basic time tracking
- Don't need data export
- Prefer community forum support
- Are just trying out Cadence

**Supporter** is worth it if you:

- Want to support development
- Value AI insights with Sage
- Enjoy being part of the community (Discord)
- Like seeing what's being built next
- Want early previews of features

**Premium Supporter** is ideal if you:

- Need data export capabilities
- Want beta/TestFlight access
- Value priority updates and notifications
- Want to influence feature prioritization
- Prefer direct feedback channels

**Feature Sponsor** is perfect if you:

- Have a specific feature you'd love to see
- Want to make a one-time contribution
- Desire lifetime community access
- Want permanent recognition

Many users find the free version sufficient for basic tracking, while supporters appreciate the community access and ability to shape Cadence's future.

## Are there any hidden fees?

No hidden fees! The price you see is what you pay:

- No setup fees
- No per-feature charges
- No usage limits or overages
- Just simple, transparent pricing

## Can I use Premium on multiple devices?

Yes! Subscriptions are linked to your Apple ID (iOS) or Google account (Android), so you can use them on:

**iOS:**

- Multiple iPhones
- Multiple iPads
- Any device signed in with your Apple ID

**Android:**

- Multiple Android phones
- Multiple Android tablets
- Any device signed in with your Google account

**Cross-platform**: Currently, iOS and Android subscriptions are separate. If you use Cadence on both platforms, you would need separate subscriptions. Cross-platform subscription support is being explored.

## What if I have billing issues?

For billing questions or issues:

**iOS (App Store):**

1. **Check your App Store subscription** first (Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions)
2. **Contact Apple Support** for payment/billing problems
3. **Email us** at [admin@cadence.day](mailto:admin@cadence.day) for account-specific questions

**Android (Google Play):**

1. **Check Google Play subscriptions** first (Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions)
2. **Contact Google Play Support** for payment/billing problems
3. **Email us** at [support@cadence.day](mailto:support@cadence.day) for account-specific questions

We're here to help!

## Do prices ever change?

While we strive to keep pricing stable, we may adjust prices for:

- New features and capabilities
- Increased AI/infrastructure costs
- Regional pricing adjustments

**Existing subscribers** are always grandfathered at their current price when they maintain continuous subscription.
